How Veritas AI Bypasses Detectors

AI detectors like Turnitin and GPTZero don't read for meaning—they measure mathematical predictability. Here is how Veritas re-engineers your text.

1. Burstiness & Cadence Injection

LLMs generate monotonous sentences with uniform syllable counts and word counts (16-24 words). Veritas breaks this mechanical symmetry by injecting punchy short sentences alongside compound structures, elevating the standard deviation.

2. Trope & Cliché Neutralization

AI models repeatedly rely on overused crutches like "delve into", "rich tapestry", "crucial role", and stiff transitions like "Furthermore,". Our linguistic catalog removes 100% of these artificial giveaways.

3. Vocabulary Entropy Expansion

By substituting predictable next-token selections with nuanced human synonyms, natural contractions, and varied syntactic openers, the Type-Token Ratio and token entropy match genuine human authorship.
