How Veritas AI Bypasses Detectors

AI detectors like Turnitin and GPTZero don't read for meaning — they measure statistical predictability and rhythmic monotony. Here is how Veritas re-engineers your prose.

STEP 01

1. Cadence & Burstiness Expansion

LLMs generate monotonous sentences with uniform word counts (16–24 words). Veritas breaks this mechanical symmetry by injecting punchy short sentences alongside complex structures, elevating the standard deviation (CV > 0.45).

Target: CV > 0.45 Burstiness
STEP 02

2. Trope & Cliché Neutralization

AI models repeatedly rely on overused crutches like "delve into", "rich tapestry", "crucial role", and stiff transitions like "Furthermore,". Our linguistic catalog removes 100% of these artificial giveaways.

0 Clichés & Robotic Fillers
STEP 03

3. Entropy & Syntactic Shift

By substituting predictable next-token selections with nuanced human synonyms, natural contractions, and varied syntactic openers, the Type-Token Ratio and token entropy match genuine human authorship.
